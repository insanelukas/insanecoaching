Rezervacijos duomenis saugau tiek, kiek reikia pokalbiui ir tolesniam bendravimui; bet kada gali paprašyti juos ištrinti. Calendly kalendorius naudoja savo slapukus ir turi atskirą slapukų pasirinkimą. Calendly privatumo pranešimas.

Jie gauna puslapio adresą, naršyklės ir įrenginio duomenis, IP adresą ir įvykių pavadinimus, pavyzdžiui, „klausimynas pradėtas“ ar „pokalbis užregistruotas“. Teisinis pagrindas: tavo sutikimas (BDAR 6 str. 1 d. a p.). Google Analytics duomenys saugomi ne ilgiau kaip 14 mėnesių.

Grįžti į puslapį

Privacy and cookies

In short: until you pick a time for a call, this page knows neither your name nor your contact details. Analytics and advertising cookies switch on only if you agree.

Who handles your data

Insane Coaching, UAB (Lithuania). Send questions and requests to lukas@insanecoaching.com (if the link does not open, copy the address).

The questions

Your answers (goal, why it matters, what you have tried, what gets in the way, when you want to start) stay in your browser. When you press “Choose a time”, they go to Calendly together with your booking as a short code, so that I can prepare for the call. The answer to the mirror question is not sent anywhere. This page does not send your answers to Google or Meta: they receive event names only.

Booking a call

You pick the time in a Calendly calendar and give your name, email and phone there. I use these only to schedule the call and to contact you. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). I keep booking data for as long as it is needed for the call and the conversation that follows; you can ask me to delete it at any time. The Calendly calendar uses its own cookies and has its own cookie choice. Calendly's privacy notice.

Cookies and analytics

Without your consent I use only what the page needs to work: your language and cookie choices are saved in your browser. If you agree, Google Analytics 4 (visitor statistics) and the Meta Pixel (to measure advertising and build advertising audiences) switch on. They receive the page address, browser and device data, your IP address and the names of events such as “quiz started” or “call booked”. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Google Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

Who receives the data

Calendly, LLC (bookings), Google Ireland Limited and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (only if you agree), and the website's hosting provider, whose servers, like all servers, record technical logs: IP address, time, browser. Some of these companies also process data in the USA, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict its use, to receive a copy, to object, and to withdraw consent. Write to lukas@insanecoaching.com and I will answer within a month. If you believe your data is handled incorrectly, you can contact the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate.
